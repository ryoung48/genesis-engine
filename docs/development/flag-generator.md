# Flag generator

Open `/experiments/flags` to browse 24 shield designs at a time. One pattern list includes geometric flag designs and **Jdenticon**, which uses the same renderer and color presets as the map overlay. **All patterns** samples from this unified list. Every variant uses the approved `world-generator` shield geometry.

**Resample flags** chooses a new batch seed. Editing **Batch seed** reproduces a batch. The URL stores `seed` and `pattern`, so it can be bookmarked or shared. Each sample derives its seed from the batch seed and its position. **Download PNG** saves that sample with transparency outside the shield.

The generator supports horizontal and vertical bicolors, tricolors and stripes; diagonal stripes; crosses and Nordic crosses; saltires, including a saltire over diagonal stripes with a contrasting outline; quarters; chevrons; diagonal bands; cantons; filled and outlined lozenges; palls; roundels and rings; grouped roundels; three rings and interlocked rings; roundels over bands; repeated dots; crescents; sunbursts; split and quartered stripes with circles or crosses; striped fields with a central circle or cross; stripes with stacked crosses; and East Asian–inspired motifs: three-comma tomoe, an off-centre rising sun with rays fanning from the base, and a centred trigram of three solid or broken bars chosen from the seed; and African-inspired motifs: woven kente-style bands of offset blocks; and ornamental motifs: an eight-pointed star from two overlapping squares with a small ring at the centre, a two-layer lotus of radial petals around a disc, and a diagonal band of two interlaced wavy strands; and an Inti-style sun disc with alternating straight and wavy rays. Circle-based designs vary their radius by seed, and preserve circular proportions within the shield. The circle over bands is centered on the middle band and has a solid interior matching that band; the upper half of its outline matches the top band and the lower half matches the bottom band. Interlocked rings alternate which ring passes over the other at the two crossings. Split stripes and crosses follows the supplied reference: vertical stripes on one side and two stacked crosses on the other, with seeded horizontal reflection. Their size and reflection vary by seed. Additional layouts inspired by the heraldry comparison reference include double chevrons, chiefs and ring bands, three-tab labels, gyronny wedges, flared and scalloped crosses. Selected simpler layouts also appear inside quarters. These variations are derived from the seed. The pattern filter holds a layout fixed while resampling its palette, orientation, and stripe count. Designs use eleven coordinated three-color palettes, optional horizontal reflection, and five or seven stripes where applicable.

**Quartered patterns** fills quarters with independently seeded existing flag designs. Some samples repeat opposite quarters; others use four different designs. Quarters have a 25% chance of being subdivided once more. **Nested quartering** guarantees at least one such subdivision. Both stop at two levels (at most 16 small fields), use a readable subset of existing layouts, and reuse the same pattern painter with rectangular clipping. Each field receives its own seeded palette, Jdenticon can appear as a field, and circles keep their proportions. No small shield outlines are drawn inside the quarters.

Rendering lives in `src/ui/heraldry/shield/`, `src/ui/heraldry/identicon/`, and `src/ui/heraldry/simple/`. The shield module owns geometry, clipping, aspect ratio, and the black outline. Identicon rendering is shared by the page and map overlay. Page state and batch generation live in `src/ui/flag-generator/`; the React view only binds controls and displays previews. Previews are rendered at 256 pixels wide when their settings change, with no animation loop.

The map continues to use identicon shields. The page allows all variants to be explored before choosing map behavior.

## Organic identicon

**Organic identicon** is a hand-cut counterpart to Jdenticon. It keeps Jdenticon's layout and colour presets: a 4×4 grid with corner, side and centre cells, repeated by four-fold rotation on a plain field. Its tiles are curved shapes with pointed tips, such as crescents, lenses, commas and wavy bands, instead of polygons. Each seed picks three different shapes and their turns, and optionally mirrors the side cells. Control points get a small seeded wobble per cell, so the four rotations are not exact copies.

The pattern appears in the generator page's list and in **All patterns**, but not inside quartered flags. It is excluded from the map subset, so map shields are unchanged. Shapes are drawn as canvas paths from `src/ui/heraldry/organic-identicon/`.

## Hue

The **Hue** setting defaults to random palettes. Selecting a hue picks palettes in a single hue family (chromatic colours within 40° of each other, plus neutrals) whose dominant colour lies within 45° of it, rotates every chromatic colour in the palette by the remaining difference so lightness and contrast are preserved, and keeps the dominant colour in one of the two colours every pattern uses. Neutral colours stay neutral. Jdenticon and organic identicon flags receive the hue directly.
 Palettes that pair a warm colour (pink, red, orange) with a green are never used, before or after rotation.

With the pattern filter on "all", half of flags are jdenticons, split evenly between the plain and organic variants, and the rest are drawn evenly from the other patterns.

The map overlay draws from an allow-list (`MAP_PATTERNS` in `src/ui/heraldry/simple/index.ts`), including inside quartered flags: jdenticon, the bicolors and tricolors, cross, diagonal stripes with saltire, the quartered layouts, chevron, double chevron, diagonal band, outlined lozenge, roundel, ring, three roundels, three rings, sunburst, split stripes with roundels, quartered stripes with crosses or roundels, striped field with cross, chief and rings, label, gyronny, trigram and the interlaced band. The generator page still offers every pattern.
