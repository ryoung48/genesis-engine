# Province wiki and local weather

A province page replaces the planet details inside the existing generation panel. It is the province detail surface. [Local weather](../climate/local-weather.md) documents the atmospheric rules and limitations; this page documents identity and navigation.

## Identity and entry points

The generated world's compact province index is canonical. Imported Earth polygon IDs map through `world.provinces.realIds`; history frames use their own `provinceMap`. Never index one space with another. Hover and clicks use the same picked longitude/latitude resolver, undoing globe transforms or flat-map recentering/latitude projection. Polygon resolution falls back to mesh membership only when the polygon or mapping is unavailable.

After active measurement/pathfinding handlers, `colorMode === "nations"` opens an owned province's owner nation from the selected historical frame. Unowned provinces fall back to their province page. Every other map mode opens the province page. Ocean/unassigned hits preserve the current page. Existing drag suppression and solar-system body picking remain separate.

Province references in nation, organization and war pages open the same page. Locate focuses/highlights without navigating away. Back returns to the originating nation, otherwise planet details. All wiki selection is mutually exclusive, and changing generated worlds clears selection. Existing hover content is unchanged in this pass.

## Contents and date

Overview shows source ID, area, representative coordinates/elevation, terrain, vegetation and climate. Society reads population, urban population, development, knowledge, culture, religion, owner and controller from the selected frame. Ownership and control are separate. A valid province needs no history initialization; missing historical information is labeled unavailable.

Weather uses the shared display clock and follows changes while the page stays open. Weather is presented only in the province wiki for this pass; no Conditions map, legend or hover weather is added. Clouds use one representative descriptive category per province, with total cover displayed numerically. Wind is a from-direction, with shared metric/imperial speed/rate formatting. Rain/snow amounts are liquid equivalent. Dew, frost, rime, wet/icy ground and snow-cover descriptions share the existing Conditions row with atmospheric events. They use a stateless 48-hour surface proxy; no separate Surface panel is added. Thunderstorms, hail, persistent blizzards, freezing rain/drizzle, ice pellets, ice crystals and haze use the same Conditions row. Thunderstorms/hail share cumulonimbus classification; haze remains a condition rather than a cloud genus. Estimated visibility and water-phase fractions remain internal numeric metadata. [Condition rules](../climate/local-weather-conditions.md) describe all approximations.

Current follows the historical date's year fraction. The reference rotation dial differs from local solar time; local day/night comes from the same illumination as the renderer. Manual overflow shows the normalized date. Annual climate displays a select-date action in the province page. Existing climate maps continue showing climate, independently of this synthetic view and of the separately toggled wind overlay.

## Presentation and lifecycle

The page uses the same muted header surface, wiki header, stat grid, section cards, entity chips and GPS action as the other wiki pages. The weather card has three tabs. **Current** has a hero row (a tinted condition icon, the condition, the temperature description with any warmer/colder-than-usual chip, and the temperature) followed by icon tiles for feels-like temperature, humidity (with dew point), wind, gusts, precipitation, sky, clouds, season and local time. Feels like is the US National Weather Service heat index at or above 80 °F (27 °C), wind chill at or below 50 °F (10 °C) with wind over 3 mph (1.3 m/s), otherwise the air temperature; humidity is relative humidity from temperature and dew point (`WEATHER_COMFORT`). Below the tiles, **Sun & moon** (`WEATHER_SKY`) lists sunrise, sunset and daylight hours, then for each of the world's moons its phase icon, phase name, illuminated fraction, moonrise, moonset and moonlight hours. Times are local solar time on the planet's own day length. Sunrise/sunset use the solar declination with the standard −0.833° refraction horizon; polar day/night and tidally locked worlds are labelled instead of timed. Moon phase and illumination follow each prepared moon's inclined, eccentric Kepler orbit, with secular nodal and apsidal precession ([eclipses](../celestial/eclipses.md#precession)). Transit uses its body-fixed longitude relative to the star; rise/set retains the instantaneous-declination and mean-rate approximation, with a +0.125° lunar horizon. Moonlight hours mean time above that horizon. The Eclipse rows show simultaneous current events and the next locally visible event (solar and lunar eclipses, moon shadows on other moons, and moon–moon occultations), with visible maximum, duration and coverage, a combined row when several moons cover the star together, an "Unavailable for …" row for moons or pairs that cannot be forecast, or "None in the next 20 years" / "Unavailable". Detailed frame, geometry and numerical search rules live in [eclipses](../celestial/eclipses.md). Daily drift in rise/set remains approximate, and gas-giant-moon worlds still treat the listed siblings as orbiting the main world. Sky and tides use the moons captured at generation. Ocean-facing provinces then show a **Sea** block with the tide level and direction, next high and low water, and significant wave height with sea state and direction ([marine conditions](../climate/marine-conditions.md)). **Hourly** shows the next 24 physical hours from the selected instant as a strip of local times, condition icons and temperatures over a temperature sparkline. **Monthly** shows the province's area-weighted monthly mean temperature and rainfall from the climate baselines, and mean wind speed sampled from the weather model 16 times per month, as bar charts with the current month highlighted. The weather worker returns this `WEATHER_OUTLOOK` with each snapshot, caching hours by exact time and months by province. Icons are Material Design Icons path data from the Pictogrammers weather category, embedded in `src/ui/components/primitives/icons/WeatherIcon.tsx` with no icon package dependency. The presenter picks the condition icon from the primary phenomenon, day/night and cloud cover; tints and card styles are `uiTokens.weather`. Sample-level debug detail and the “Simulated province weather” footer are omitted. Descriptive temperature, wind and precipitation wording accompanies the existing numeric values. Quiet downpours, thin mist, tiny flakes and unusual-temperature markers use the shared presenter. The season row follows hemisphere and orbital longitude; local time includes dawn/morning/afternoon/dusk/night or polar/locked-world illumination wording. No new drawer or map overlay is added. The application continues to request 204000 world points; reduced-size smoke-test fixtures do not change that setting.

Exact-time wiki snapshots show loading rather than relabeling old results. Cloud absence is distinct from missing cloud data. Initialization errors are visible; regeneration terminates the dedicated weather worker and invalidates replies. Weather operates independently of the separate generation/history worker. Neither weather nor this navigation changes historical outcomes.

Runtime verification artifacts and diagnostic reports stay in the ignored `stats/weather/` directory. Use a generated world for screenshots; an empty scene does not verify province picking, page contents or weather execution.
